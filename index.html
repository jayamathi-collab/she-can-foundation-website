Our Story

Who We Are

She Can Foundation was born from a simple belief: every woman, every girl, every dreamer deserves a fair chance. We bridge the gap between potential and opportunity through education, mentorship, and community-driven programs.

From remote villages to urban classrooms, we are present wherever courage meets possibility. Our volunteers, mentors, and donors create ecosystems of growth — one life, one story, one dream at a time.

Recognition & Trust

Our Credentials

She Can Foundation is a registered non-profit committed to transparency, accountability, and measurable impact.

🏛️ Government Registered

NGO Registration Certificate

Registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860. Recognized by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs as a legitimate non-profit organization operating across India.

💜 80G Tax Exemption

Income Tax Exemption

Donations to She Can Foundation are eligible for 80G tax deductions under the Income Tax Act, making every contribution even more rewarding for our donors.

🌐 International Presence

Global Outreach Program

Partnered with UN-affiliated organizations and international NGO networks to expand digital education and women empowerment programs beyond India's borders.

⭐ Award Recognized

Excellence in Social Impact

Recipient of the Youth Social Entrepreneur Award 2023 for outstanding contribution to women's education and community empowerment in South India.
